# Gin Rummy: Crimson Royale — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 24 November 2026**

**Gin Rummy: Crimson Royale** is developed and published by **Dizzoo Studios** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Information We Handle

### a) Game Progress and Settings

The game stores information locally on your device to support gameplay, including:

- Player levels, experience points (XP), and game progression.
- Unlocked Gin Rummy tables and available game modes.
- Virtual coin balances, match rewards, and transaction history.
- Daily rewards and reward claim history.
- Game preferences, including sound, music, and other settings.
- Locally stored gameplay information needed to maintain game functionality.

This information allows the game to restore your progress and preferences between sessions.

The offline version of the game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play.

### b) Advertising

The game may use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements, where these features are enabled.

Depending on your device, advertising settings, and applicable permissions, Google's advertising services may collect or process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- App interactions, including advertisement views and taps.
- Diagnostic information, including crash and performance data.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services.

For more information, see [Google's advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### c) Support Requests

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond to your questions, investigate reported issues, and provide game-related support.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy is used to:

- Save and restore Gin Rummy progress and preferences.
- Maintain player levels, experience points, and unlocked tables.
- Manage virtual coins, match rewards, and daily rewards.
- Support offline gameplay against AI opponents.
- Display advertisements and verify rewarded-ad completion, where applicable.
- Maintain game functionality and troubleshoot technical issues.
- Respond to player support requests.
- Help advertising providers measure performance and prevent abuse.

## 3. Notifications

If notification features are enabled in the game, it may request permission to send reminders about daily rewards or other game-related activities.

Local notifications are scheduled on your device and do not require you to provide personal contact information.

You can allow or disable notifications through your device's settings.

## 4. In-Game Currency

Gin Rummy: Crimson Royale includes a virtual in-game currency called **Coins**.

Players can earn or use virtual coins through supported game features, including:

- Completing and winning Gin Rummy matches.
- Receiving match rewards.
- Claiming daily rewards.
- Playing at different Gin Rummy tables.
- Receiving optional rewarded-ad bonuses, where available.

Virtual coins are intended solely for entertainment and gameplay purposes.

**Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, transferred, or redeemed for real money or cash prizes.**

The current version does not offer real-money wagering, cash withdrawals, or real-money prizes.

The game does not collect payment-card information through its offline gameplay.
